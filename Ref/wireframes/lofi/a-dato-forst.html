A · Dato først (date first)

The visitor tells us when and where before browsing. Every list then shows only what can arrive on that day, at a known delivery fee. Modelled on the florist practice in P2 (postcode and date first).

Strong for the gift sender and the occasion buyer under time pressure. Risk: one extra step before any flowers are shown, which may cost Instagram visitors who only want to look.
