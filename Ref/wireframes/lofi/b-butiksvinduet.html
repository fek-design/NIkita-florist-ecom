B · Butiksvinduet (the shop window)

Photos lead, as on the shop's Instagram today. The visitor browses freely, then picks the date and checks the postcode on the product page, before the cart. Checkout is three short steps.

Strong for visitors arriving from an Instagram post and for the local regular. Risk: the delivery constraint is met later, so a visitor can fall for a bouquet that cannot arrive in time. The product page has to catch that early.
