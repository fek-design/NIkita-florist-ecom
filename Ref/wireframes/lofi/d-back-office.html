D · Back office (the owner's side)

What the florist sees, on her phone, between two bouquets. The same for all three storefront directions. The screens follow her day: orders first, then product upkeep, then settings and sales.

Works for both product models. Fixed bouquets live in screens 4–5. "Buket efter budget" (her phone sale: occasion + budget, she designs) lives in screen 6. The notes say what drops out if the storefront ends up with only one of them.

The test for every screen: does it take less of her time than the phone call it replaces? Time costs are in the notes.
