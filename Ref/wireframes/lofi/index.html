Lo-fi wireframes: three directions

Five key screens each (home, category, product, cart, checkout) at 390 px, mobile first (P1). Greyscale on purpose: these compare structure and flow, not looks. Screen copy is Danish placeholder text; prices, products and shop details are invented placeholders until the owner interview.

The owner's side

D · Back officeSeven phone screens in the order of her day: log ind, ordrer i dag, ordre, produkter, ret produkt, buketter & kapacitet, salg. The same for A, B and C.For: the florist, between two bouquets.
"Hjemmesiden tager telefonen for dig."Her main sale today is a phone call: occasion + budget, she designs. A web order asks the same questions, is paid up front, and waits until her hands are free. The back office must take less time than the calls it replaces.

Questions for the owner about the back office

  • Budget bouquets, a catalogue of fixed bouquets, or both? (Decides how much of screens 4–6 she maintains.)
  • Does she want a sale price (tilbudspris) per product? Discount codes are out of scope.
  • Does anyone else use the admin (staff, a courier)?
  • How should new orders reach her: email only, or also SMS/push?

Needed before the next fidelity step

Lo-fi needs nothing from the brand guide. These are what the mid-fi wireframes and the owner review need.

From the owner (content and rules)

  • Product range: categories, rough count, sizes and price levels; which items are perishable
  • Occasions she actually sells for (and whether funeral work is a big part)
  • Delivery: area (postcodes), fee(s), cut-off time, delivery window, closed days, church/chapel delivery
  • Pickup: yes/no, hours
  • Add-ons sold (vases, cards, chocolate)?
  • Real shop details as approved: name, address, CVR, phone, email, opening hours
  • Does she want phone calls from customers?

From the brand guide (change establish-brand-identity)

  • Page ground: white or paper (decision 5)
  • Colour roles: which token for text, buttons, bands, footer; the allowed pairings with contrast
  • Heading and body typefaces, with the type scale (5–7 steps) for 390 px
  • Logo use on the site: which file of the existing logo, minimum size in the header, the phone line removed or hidden
  • Imagery direction: photo ratio (4:5 or 1:1), background, one consistent style
  • Spacing and radius scale, button style (filled ink, radius)
  • Voice: a few rules and sample lines in Danish (headings, buttons, errors)
