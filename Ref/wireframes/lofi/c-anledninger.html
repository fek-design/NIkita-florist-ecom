C · Anledninger (occasions)

The visitor starts from why they buy: a birthday, a thank-you, a funeral. Each occasion page is a calm, factual guide with a short range and answers to the usual questions. Delivery to a church or chapel is built in.

Strong for the occasion buyer (sympathy, weddings) and for SEO/GEO, since occasion pages answer what people ask. Risk: needs written content per occasion from the owner, and a visitor who "just wants flowers" has one more choice to make.
