## What it is

The first complete hi-fi set, made from proven patterns (Baymard, Shopify Polaris) and Felix's competitor boards, on top of Felix's front page and the decisions DD-01 to DD-15.

- Storefront: S1 front page, S2 menu, S3 category page, S4 occasion page, S4b postcode outside the area, S5 added-to-cart sheet, S6 cart, S7 checkout, S8 confirmation.
- Back office: B1 sign-in (no content yet), B2 orders, B3 order detail, B4 occasions, B5 edit occasion, B6 delivery and capacity. B7 sales was not built.

## Why it was frozen

Frozen early, before Felix's full review: while the back office was being built, Felix brought in the owner's way of working (paper, a pinboard, as little screen time as possible). The back office in v1 assumes she manages orders on her phone all day. v1 is kept as the record of that assumption; v2 changes it.

## Known gaps in v1

- B1 sign-in has a title only; B7 sales is missing.
- Delivery has no time window and pickup has no time; customers get no word on when to expect the bouquet.
- Stand-in photos from Felix's mockup; Tak has no photo.
